Best Places to Visit in Ella, Sri Lanka

Discover mountain views, tea plantations, waterfalls and unforgettable places to explore in Ella.

Ella is one of Sri Lanka's most beautiful hill-country destinations. Surrounded by green mountains, tea estates, forests and valleys, the town is a favourite among travellers and photographers.

Here are some of the best places to visit when exploring Ella and its surrounding area.

01

Little Adam's Peak

Little Adam's Peak is one of the easiest viewpoints to reach around Ella. The short hike leads to beautiful panoramic views of the surrounding mountains and valleys.

Best for: Sunrise, sunset and mountain photography.
02

Nine Arch Bridge

The Nine Arch Bridge is one of Ella's most recognizable landmarks. The historic railway bridge is surrounded by lush greenery and tea-country scenery.

Best for: Photography and watching trains pass through the bridge.
03

Ella Rock

Ella Rock offers a more challenging hiking experience with rewarding views across the surrounding highlands.

The trail passes through natural landscapes and offers an excellent opportunity to experience Ella's mountain environment.

Best for: Hiking, adventure and panoramic views.
04

Ravana Falls

Ravana Falls is a popular waterfall located close to Ella. Water flows over a rocky formation surrounded by green hills and vegetation.

Best for: Nature photography and a short scenic stop.
05

Ella Tea Plantations

The hills around Ella are covered with tea plantations that create some of the most beautiful landscapes in Sri Lanka's hill country.

Walking through the surrounding tea country is a great way to experience the scenery and learn more about Sri Lanka's famous tea-growing regions.

Best for: Landscapes, photography and relaxing walks.
06

Ella Spice Garden

Visitors interested in Sri Lankan plants, spices and traditional uses can explore gardens and local experiences around the Ella area.

Best for: Learning about local plants and spices.
07

Demodara Railway Area

The railway scenery around Demodara is another interesting attraction for photographers and railway enthusiasts. The surrounding hills make the area especially scenic.

Best for: Railway photography and scenic views.
08

Ella Town

Ella town itself is surrounded by restaurants, cafés, small shops and accommodation options. It is a convenient base for exploring the surrounding mountains and attractions.

Best for: Food, relaxing and exploring local life.

Why Visit Ella?

Ella offers a combination of mountains, forests, tea plantations, waterfalls and railway scenery that makes it one of Sri Lanka's most photogenic destinations.

Whether you enjoy hiking, photography, nature or simply relaxing among the hills, Ella has something memorable to offer.
