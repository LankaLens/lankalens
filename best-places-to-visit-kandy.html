10 Best Places to Visit in Kandy, Sri Lanka

Discover culture, history, beautiful landscapes and unforgettable experiences in the heart of Sri Lanka.

Kandy is one of Sri Lanka's most important cultural destinations. Surrounded by green hills and centred around the famous Kandy Lake, the city offers a mix of history, religion, nature and local life.

If you are planning a trip to Kandy, here are 10 places worth adding to your itinerary.

01

Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic is one of Kandy's most important cultural and religious landmarks. It is located near Kandy Lake in the historic centre of the city.

Best for: Culture, history and architecture.
02

Kandy Lake

Kandy Lake is located in the centre of the city and is surrounded by many of Kandy's most famous attractions. A walk around the lake offers beautiful views of the surrounding hills.

Best for: Walking, photography and relaxing.
03

Royal Botanic Gardens, Peradeniya

The Royal Botanic Gardens in Peradeniya are known for their extensive collection of plants, trees and flowers. The gardens provide a peaceful escape close to Kandy.

Best for: Nature, gardens and photography.
04

Bahirawakanda Temple

Located on a hill above Kandy, this temple area provides impressive views across the city and surrounding landscape.

Best for: City views and photography.
05

Kandy View Point

Kandy View Point is a popular location for seeing the city from above. The surrounding hills and buildings create a memorable landscape, especially around sunrise and sunset.

Best for: Panoramic views and photography.
06

Udawattakele Forest Reserve

Udawattakele is a forest reserve located close to central Kandy. Its trails provide an opportunity to experience greenery and wildlife without travelling far from the city.

Best for: Nature walks and bird watching.
07

World Buddhist Museum

Visitors interested in Buddhist history and culture can explore museums and cultural sites around the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic area.

Best for: History and cultural learning.
08

Kandy Esala Perahera

The Kandy Esala Perahera is one of Sri Lanka's most famous cultural festivals. The event features traditional performances, dancers, drummers and a spectacular procession.

Best for: Culture, photography and traditional performances.
09

Hanthana Mountain Range

The Hanthana hills provide scenic landscapes around Kandy. The area is attractive for travellers who enjoy hiking, green mountain scenery and photography.

Best for: Hiking, mountains and nature.
10

Kandy City

Don't overlook Kandy itself. Exploring the streets, markets, cafés and local shops is a great way to experience everyday life in this historic hill-country city.

Best for: Local food, shopping and exploring.

Why Visit Kandy?

Kandy offers a unique combination of Sri Lankan culture, history, nature and hill-country scenery. It is also a convenient base for exploring other parts of central Sri Lanka.

From peaceful gardens and mountain views to historic temples and busy city streets, Kandy has plenty to discover.
