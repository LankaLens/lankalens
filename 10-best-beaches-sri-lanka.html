10 Best Beaches in Sri Lanka

Discover beautiful beaches, tropical coastlines and unforgettable seaside destinations across Sri Lanka.

Sri Lanka is surrounded by the Indian Ocean and has a coastline filled with beautiful beaches. Different parts of the island offer different experiences, from peaceful swimming spots and palm-lined shores to surfing destinations and lively coastal towns.

Here are 10 beaches worth exploring when travelling around Sri Lanka.

01

Mirissa Beach

Mirissa is one of the most popular beaches on Sri Lanka's southern coast. Its curved sandy beach, palm trees and relaxed atmosphere make it a beautiful place to enjoy the ocean.

Best for: Sunsets, relaxing and coastal photography.
02

Unawatuna Beach

Located near Galle, Unawatuna is known for its tropical coastal scenery and warm waters. The surrounding area also offers restaurants, accommodation and other attractions.

Best for: Swimming, relaxing and exploring the south coast.
03

Arugam Bay

Arugam Bay on the east coast is internationally known as a surfing destination. The area has a relaxed atmosphere and a long coastline that attracts surfers and travellers.

Best for: Surfing and adventure.
04

Nilaveli Beach

Nilaveli, near Trincomalee, offers a beautiful stretch of sandy coastline with a more peaceful atmosphere. The area is especially attractive for travellers looking for a relaxing beach escape.

Best for: Quiet beach days and ocean views.
05

Hikkaduwa Beach

Hikkaduwa is a lively beach destination on the southwest coast. It is popular for its coastal atmosphere, water activities and nearby restaurants and accommodation.

Best for: Water activities and a lively coastal experience.
06

Bentota Beach

Bentota is a well-known coastal destination with a long sandy shoreline and a variety of activities. It is also a popular area for travellers looking for beach resorts and water-based experiences.

Best for: Resorts, relaxation and water activities.
07

Pasikuda Beach

Pasikuda on Sri Lanka's eastern coast is known for its beautiful shallow waters and wide sandy beach. It is a peaceful destination for enjoying the tropical coastline.

Best for: Relaxing and enjoying shallow coastal waters.
08

Weligama Beach

Weligama is another popular destination on the southern coast. Its wide bay and surfing conditions make it a favourite among both beginners and experienced travellers.

Best for: Surfing and learning to surf.
09

Tangalle Beach

Tangalle offers beautiful coastal scenery with sandy beaches, palm trees and a quieter atmosphere compared with some of the busier southern beach towns.

Best for: Peaceful coastal scenery and photography.
10

Talpe Beach

Talpe is a quieter coastal area near Galle. Its rocky coastline, palm trees and peaceful surroundings can make it an interesting place for photographers and travellers looking for less crowded scenery.

Best for: Photography and peaceful coastal views.

Explore More of Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka has many more beaches and coastal destinations waiting to be discovered. From the southern coastline to the east and north, every region offers its own character and beauty.
