10 Beautiful Places in Sri Lanka You Should Visit

Discover beaches, mountains, historic cities and unforgettable natural landscapes across the island.

Sri Lanka is a small island with an incredible variety of landscapes and experiences. Within a relatively short distance, travellers can discover tropical beaches, misty mountains, ancient cities, wildlife and vibrant local culture.

Here are 10 beautiful places that deserve a place on your Sri Lanka travel list.

01

Ella

Surrounded by green mountains, tea plantations and dramatic valleys, Ella is one of Sri Lanka's most popular highland destinations.

The area is especially beautiful for photography, hiking and enjoying cool mountain scenery.

02

Galle

Galle combines Sri Lankan coastal scenery with centuries of history. The famous Galle Fort is filled with old streets, colonial architecture, cafés and ocean views.

03

Mirissa

Mirissa is known for its beautiful beach, palm trees and relaxed coastal atmosphere.

It is a great destination for travellers looking for sunsets, beaches and ocean experiences.

04

Nuwara Eliya

Often associated with Sri Lanka's tea country, Nuwara Eliya offers cool weather, green hills, tea plantations and beautiful landscapes.

05

Kandy

Located among green hills, Kandy is one of Sri Lanka's most important cultural destinations.

The city is also known for its lake, surrounding mountains and rich cultural heritage.

06

Sigiriya

Sigiriya is home to the famous ancient rock fortress rising dramatically above the surrounding landscape.

The combination of history, architecture and natural scenery makes it one of Sri Lanka's most iconic places.

07

Arugam Bay

Arugam Bay is a popular destination on Sri Lanka's eastern coast, especially among surfers and beach lovers.

Its relaxed atmosphere and coastal scenery make it a memorable place to explore.

08

Horton Plains

Horton Plains is a highland landscape filled with grasslands, forests, mist and dramatic viewpoints.

The area is particularly attractive for hikers and nature lovers.

09

Unawatuna

Unawatuna is a popular southern beach destination surrounded by tropical scenery.

Its warm waters, sandy coastline and nearby attractions make it a favourite coastal stop.

10

Jaffna

Jaffna offers a different side of Sri Lanka, with distinctive northern culture, architecture, food and landscapes.

It is an interesting destination for travellers who want to experience more of the island beyond the traditional tourist routes.

Explore More of Sri Lanka

These destinations are only a small part of what Sri Lanka has to offer. From hidden beaches and waterfalls to mountain villages and local food, there are countless stories waiting to be photographed.
